How it works

Four steps to mastery

Your teacher conducts a live class on Google Meet — TamGam does the rest automatically.

01
📹

Attend live class

Join your teacher's live class on Google Meet. TamGam captures the transcript automatically in the background.

02
📝

AI generates notes

Gemini reads the transcript and creates beautiful, structured notes from your class — ready within minutes.

03
🧪

Adaptive assessment

Take a personalised quiz based on exactly what was taught. Questions adjust to your level automatically.

04

Ask Diya anything

Stuck on something? Diya, your AI tutor, answers questions grounded in your class content — at your level.

📖

AI-generated notes

Structured, chapter-wise notes created from every live class transcript by Gemini 2.5 Flash.

🎯

Adaptive assessments

Questions calibrated to your understanding level (1–5). Get harder as you improve, easier when you struggle.
